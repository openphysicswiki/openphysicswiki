## Statement
At least one inertial reference frame exists: there is at least one frame satisfying the free-particle criterion.

## Assumptions
Newtonian space and time and sufficiently isolated test particles are assumed. Uniform free-particle motion defines the frame criterion; this axiom adds the existence claim.
