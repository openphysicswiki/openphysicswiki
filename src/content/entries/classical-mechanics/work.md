## Statement
The work done by a force $\mathbf F$ on a point particle along trajectory $C$ from $\mathbf r_1$ to $\mathbf r_2$ is
$$
W_C=\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r
=\int_{t_1}^{t_2}\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf v\,dt.
$$
Net work is obtained by taking $\mathbf F=\mathbf F_{\mathrm{net}}$.

## Assumptions
The force and velocity are evaluated along the same piecewise-smooth trajectory in a specified reference frame, and the integral exists; the force need not be conservative.
