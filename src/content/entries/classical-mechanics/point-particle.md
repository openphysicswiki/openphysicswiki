## Statement
A point particle is a body whose configuration at time $t$ is represented by a single position vector $\mathbf r(t)$. It may carry properties such as mass $m$, but it has no orientation or deformation variables.

## Assumptions
The body's size, shape, and internal motion are negligible on the scales of interest; a reference frame and time coordinate are specified.
