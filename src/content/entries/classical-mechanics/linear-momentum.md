## Statement
The linear momentum of a point particle with instantaneous inertial mass $m$ and velocity $\mathbf v$ is
$$
\mathbf p=m\mathbf v.
$$

## Assumptions
The particle has a well-defined mass $m>0$, its velocity is measured in a specified reference frame, and its speed is nonrelativistic.
