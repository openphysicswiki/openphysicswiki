## Statement
The kinetic energy of a point particle of mass $m$ moving with velocity $\mathbf v$ is
$$
T=\frac12 m v^2=\frac12 m\,\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf v,
\qquad v=\lVert\mathbf v\rVert.
$$

## Assumptions
The particle has constant mass $m>0$, its velocity is measured in a specified reference frame, and its speed is nonrelativistic.
