## Statement
For a point particle with trajectory $\mathbf r(t)$, its velocity $\mathbf v$ and acceleration $\mathbf a$ are
$$
\mathbf v(t)=\frac{d\mathbf r}{dt},
\qquad
\mathbf a(t)=\frac{d\mathbf v}{dt}=\frac{d^2\mathbf r}{dt^2}.
$$

## Assumptions
All derivatives are taken with respect to the same time coordinate in a fixed reference frame, and $\mathbf r(t)$ is sufficiently differentiable.
