## Statement
An inertial frame is a reference frame in which every free test point particle has $\mathbf a=\mathbf 0$, equivalently constant velocity $\mathbf v$. A free particle has zero net external force.

## Assumptions
Zero net external force is established from the particle's interactions, independently of its observed acceleration; inferring it from $\mathbf a=\mathbf 0$ would make the criterion circular. Acceleration is measured relative to the candidate frame using a common Newtonian time coordinate.
