## Statement
If point particle $B$ exerts force $\mathbf F_{A\leftarrow B}$ on point particle $A$, then $A$ exerts force $\mathbf F_{B\leftarrow A}$ on $B$, with
$$
\mathbf F_{A\leftarrow B}=-\mathbf F_{B\leftarrow A}.
$$

## Assumptions
Both forces denote only the same pairwise interaction and are evaluated at the same Newtonian time; they need not be the total forces on either particle. This weak form asserts neither collinearity with the particle separation nor cancellation of virtual work for arbitrary displacements.
